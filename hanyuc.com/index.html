Publications Teaching Listening

I am a third-year Ph.D. student in Computer Science at Cornell University, advised by Professor Noah Snavely. Before starting my Ph.D., I received my M.S. and B.S. in Computer Science from Carnegie Mellon University, where I was advised by Professor Ioannis Gkioulekas. My research interest lies in differentiable and neural rendering for 3D reconstruction, and more broadly at the intersection of graphics and 3D vision.
